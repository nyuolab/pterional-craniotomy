These are educational atlas scenes with original illustrative bone openings, a local dural envelope and schematic motion. They are not article-specific reconstructions, validated operative simulations, or patient models. Deep fascial laminae and three fat pads are shown as original smooth illustrations fitted to the atlas. Their geometry is not a native segmentation. Arachnoid, facial nerve branches and perforators are not modeled. Colored markings, incision paths, burr holes, closure symbols and directional arrows are original schematic illustrations fitted to the reference anatomy. The scalp, head frame and sterile drapes are not reconstructed; markings on the outer reference surfaces do not establish a measured skin trajectory. The left dural patch is fitted to native left bone and cortex. Other left procedural layers and tools are mirrored illustrations; native left-sided atlas structures retain their own geometry. Cyan instruments show qualitative entry directions rather than measured trajectories. The clinoid detail isolates the sphenoid, arteries and nerves; calvaria, local lateral dura and cortex are omitted in this view, not removed by clinoidectomy. Full head context expands the enabled layers to the available head atlas structures. Each layer can also be expanded separately with its All checkbox; this does not imply complete segmentation of every tissue. Amber marks the current illustrative change; Before/After preserves preceding steps.

The muscle-splitting view retains intact reference muscle; an exact fiber split is not reconstructed. The plane diagram is an original schematic, not a published figure. Article text and bibliographic references are available in Papers.
